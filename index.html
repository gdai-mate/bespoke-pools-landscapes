Why Bespoke

Built around keeping you in the loop.

A pool is a big build with a lot of moving parts. The thing our clients thank us for most is simple: they always knew where things were at. We keep you posted the whole way, so it feels handled from the first meeting to handover.

One point of contact

You deal directly with the people building your pool. Owners on the tools, on your site, reachable when you need them.

Regular updates

The crew

A small, hands-on team, led by the owners.

Bespoke is run by Josh Kennaway and Matt Fittell, both on the tools, both across every job. Around them is a tight crew who've been building pools and gardens across Brisbane for years. You'll know the faces on your site.
