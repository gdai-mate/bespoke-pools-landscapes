Brisbane inner & eastern suburbs

Small yards, big results.

Inner-city backyards are rarely big or flat, and getting machinery in can mean a narrow side path or a crane over the roof. Many homes are Queenslanders and character houses, so the pool, fencing and garden need to sit comfortably with the house. We design to the block, engineer for the slope, and choose between a custom concrete pool or a craned-in Plungie depending on access.
