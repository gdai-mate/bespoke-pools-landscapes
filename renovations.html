Good to know

Pool renovations in Brisbane, straight answers.

  • The interior is rough, stained, flaking or hard to keep clean
  • Tiles or coping are cracked, drummy or dated
  • The pump, filter or lighting is old and costly to run
  • The pool fence won't pass a pool safety inspection
  • The surrounding paving, deck or garden has had its day
