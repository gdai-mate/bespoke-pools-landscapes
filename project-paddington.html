Project · Paddington, BrisbaneA tropical retreat behind a Queenslander.

Pool, paving, feature walls, seating, water feature and garden, designed as one space and built by one team.

The project

One backyard, designed as a whole.

The brief was a backyard that felt like a holiday without leaving Paddington. We set a rectangular pool with a deep blue mosaic interior into travertine paving, with frameless glass so the water stays the hero from the house and the lawn.

Around it we built white breeze-block feature walls with travertine-capped bench seating on a tiled base, a raised tiled water trough, and a lawn that ties it all together. Tropical planting does the screening: pandanus, palms, bamboo and heliconia, so the yard feels private and lush within a season.

Down the side of the Queenslander, timber stairs, stepping-stone paths and garden beds carry the same look right around the house.
