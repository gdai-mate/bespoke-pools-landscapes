Good to know

Building a pool in Brisbane, the honest answers.

Three kinds. Fully custom concrete pools designed from scratch for your block, Plungie plunge pools that are precast and craned in, and pool renovations that bring an old pool back. Whichever way you go, we build the fencing, paving, decking and garden around it too.

The biggest levers are size and shape, how easy it is to get machinery into the yard, what's under the ground (slope, soil, rock), the interior and coping finishes, and how much of the surrounding backyard is part of the job. A plunge pool on a flat, open site sits at one end. A custom concrete pool with retaining, glass fencing and full landscaping sits at the other.

We quote a clear fixed price once we've seen the block, and we walk you through what's driving the number so nothing turns up later.

A custom concrete pool is a proper build with a four to six week cure in the middle, so allow a few months from approval to first swim depending on the scope. A Plungie is far quicker, because the shell is made off-site and craned in. Either way we give you a realistic timeline up front and keep you posted through the build.
