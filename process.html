Step by step

You'll always know what's next.

A pool is a real build, with real stages. We keep you in the loop at each one, so it always feels handled from the first meeting to the first swim.

01

Design & quote about a week

We meet, look at your block, and design a pool and backyard to suit it. You get a clear concept and a quote with no mystery line items.

02

Contract & engineering a few weeks

We lock in the plan, then our engineers work out the structure for your soil and slope.

03

Council & certification a week to a few months

We handle every approval and certification for you. This is the one stage we can't put a firm clock on. It depends on your block, any overlays and how busy council is. We keep it moving and keep you posted.

04

Dig & steel about a week

Excavation and the steel-reinforced shell. This is where it starts to feel real.

05

Concrete & cure four to six weeks

We pour and then let it cure properly. Worth the wait for a shell that lasts. (Chasing something faster? A plunge pool skips this.)

06

Tiling & fencing

Waterline tiling, coping and finishes, then your compliant glass fencing goes in.

07

Interior & landscaping

The pool interior, then decking, paving, gardens and lighting. The softscaping goes in last, so the whole space lands finished.

08

Handover & care about a day

We walk you through your finished pool and hand over your warranties and care guides. For ongoing pool care and water treatment, we point you to a specialist we trust, so it's always in good hands.
