Why a Plungie

The fast way to a proper pool.

A custom concrete pool is worth the wait, but a Plungie is on average around 74% faster than a traditional in-situ pool. It arrives as a single-pour, award-winning shell with no joins, then we handle the rest.

  • Delivered and craned in, less disruption to your yard
  • In-ground, aboveground or semi-recessed, ideal for tight or sloping blocks
  • Backed by a 10-year warranty and independently audited concrete testing
  • Finished by us with fencing, decking, paving and landscaping
